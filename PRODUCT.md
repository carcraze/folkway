## Product
Folkway (`folkway.io`) — an autonomous marketing platform for ecommerce and small businesses with **no marketing team**. It connects to a store's live catalog, plugs conversion and ad-spend leaks, and generates ready-to-run ads, social posts and email flows from the merchant's **real SKUs**. Behind the hood it is sold and delivered as an agency (human founder face, AI engine underneath).

## Buyer
- Shopify/WooCommerce founders doing $5k–$100k/mo alone (fulfillment, support, ads all on one person).
- Performance advertisers spending $50–$500/day on Meta/Google fighting creative fatigue.
- Independent brands and local/service operators who can't justify a $3,500/mo agency retainer.
- Geography: UK/US/CA/AU primarily. `[A]`

## Job the page must do (in under 15 seconds)
1. Confirm the category: autonomous marketing platform, not a generic AI social poster.
2. Convert on the spot with a zero-friction wedge: store URL + work email → Free 1-Page Storefront & Ad Leak Audit + 3 custom product creatives.
3. Anchor transparent pricing with the responsibility-based ladder and a 30-day money-back guarantee.
